# Editing the site

Go to **8twgb9k22v-sketch.github.io/ed-eastham/admin** (later edeastham.co.uk/admin), press **Login with Netlify Identity**, and use your email and password. Works on Mac, Windows and phone; a laptop is more comfortable.

Everything saves with **Publish → Publish now**. The live site (**8twgb9k22v-sketch.github.io/ed-eastham**, later edeastham.co.uk) updates about a minute later; refresh the page to see it. Nothing is ever lost: every publish is kept in history.

## The easy way: Studio (edit the site by clicking on it)

Go to **8twgb9k22v-sketch.github.io/ed-eastham/edit** (later edeastham.co.uk/edit) and sign in
with the same email and password. You see your real site with a toolbar around it.

- **Click any title, caption or paragraph and type.** Enter or clicking away keeps it, Esc cancels.
- **Click a picture** for its options: Change picture, Details (title, medium, size, year, price,
  sold), Move up / Move down, Delete. Or fill in the panel on the right.
- **Drag a work** up or down within its category to reorder.
- **Left rail:** Add work, Pages (rename categories, intros, order), Design (colours, typeface,
  logo, menu labels, footer line), Media (every picture you've uploaded).
- **Top bar:** page switcher, Undo / Redo, Phone (see it at phone width), Preview (hide the
  editing tools), **Publish**.
- Your work is **saved as a draft on this device as you go** ("Saved" next to Publish). Nothing
  goes live until you press Publish. If you close the tab, Studio offers to restore the draft next
  time. If someone changed the site meanwhile, Publish shows you what changed and lets you keep
  yours or take theirs, per item.
- After Publish the site updates in about a minute.

The form-based editor at `/admin` still works if you prefer it.

### Move, resize and style components

Open **Components**, then click a heading, picture, button, container, or section on the page.
The searchable list also includes hidden components. **Select container** selects the whole
group around an item; use it to move an entire section instead of only its heading.

- **Move before / Move after** changes the order inside its container. **Move to another
  container** places an individual component in a different section. Artwork stays attached
  to its details: change its **Portfolio category** in Work properties to move it to another page.
- **Computer / Phone** chooses which screen size you are styling. Change width, alignment,
  spacing, text size, colours, borders, picture fit and focus. Computer also has fine position
  controls. Phone styles are separate. Changing section order applies on both screen sizes.
- **Arrange contents** stacks items or puts them in columns. Blank fields use the original
  design. **Reset this screen’s styles** removes the selected component’s styling for that
  screen; Undo reverses moves and text changes too.
- Edit text and link addresses in the panel. Images can be uploaded or picked from existing
  media. **Hide on phone / computer** hides an item without deleting its content.

Open **Build page** for the current page’s paragraphs, exhibitions, commission steps and button
text. On Home, choose rotating artwork and toggle sections. **Site details & contact** edits
the artist name, tagline, email, Instagram and location; social links can be added or removed.

All these changes use the same local drafts, Undo / Redo and Publish button as normal edits.
Always preview both screen sizes before publishing, especially after moving components.

### Fill an empty portfolio page

Open a category such as Pen & Ink. In Studio, the empty page shows **Add artwork**, **Upload
several**, **Add image**, and **Add text box**. These buttons appear only in the editor.

- **Add artwork** selects the current category for you. **Upload several** creates a separate
  artwork for every picture, with editable titles and details. If a picture cannot be opened,
  completed uploads stay in the draft and retrying skips them.
- **Build page** offers Large images, Two columns and Three columns for the gallery.
  These presets keep a single column on phones.
- **Your artworks** lists every artwork on the current portfolio page with its thumbnail.
  Expand one to rename it, edit details, change its category or picture, reorder or remove it.
  Undo restores removals, and the original picture stays in Media.
- **Add image / Add text box** adds standalone blocks above or below the artwork. Pick an
  existing picture by thumbnail, or upload a new one. Add headings, captions and descriptions.
- Expand a block under **Your page blocks** to edit, move, duplicate or remove it. Undo can
  recover a removed block. These blocks work on the other site pages too.
- Click a picture, then **Size & placement** for a size slider, left/centre/right placement,
  Original/Square/Portrait/Wide framing and a crop-focus preview. Use Move earlier/later to
  reorder artwork or image blocks. Computer and Phone controls remain separate.

Images are checked before they enter the draft. Use PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 25 MB each.

## Works
**Works** in the left column lists every piece with its picture. Use **Filter** at the top to see one category. **New Work**: type the title, drop the photo on the Picture box, pick the Category, add medium, size and year. **Price** lists it in the Shop; **Sold** marks it sold. **Order** decides where it sits on its page (1 first). To change a picture, open the work and choose a new one.

Photos straight from a phone are fine: they are shrunk for the web automatically when the site publishes. Save them as JPEG, not HEIC.

## Portfolio categories
Rename a category, add an intro line, or reorder the menu. **New Category** adds a page and a menu entry. If you delete a category that still has works in it, those works are not lost: they appear on an **Other works** page until you file them under another category.

## Pages
About (bio, picture, exhibitions), Commission (text, button, steps), Shop text, Contact text. One box per paragraph.

## Site settings
- **Site details**: name, tagline, email (used by every Enquire button and the contact form), Instagram, location.
- **Home page**: which works rotate beside the logo, and which blocks show.
- **Look and feel**: colours, typeface, your own logo, menu labels, social links, footer line.

If something looks wrong after publishing, open it again and check the field; nothing is lost, every change is kept in history.
